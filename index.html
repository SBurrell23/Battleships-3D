        COMBAT BRIEFING

        DEPLOY

        Anchor every hull inside your own waters. Pick a hull from the registry, aim, press R to swing it broadside, then click to set. Click an anchored hull to lift it again.

        ENGAGE

        On your turn, click any unfired tile in enemy waters. A shell arcs over the strait. A water plume means a miss; fire and debris means you struck steel.

        OBSERVE

        Keys 1-4 swap between overview, your waters, enemy waters and free cam. Drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, right-drag to pan. In free cam use WASD and Q/E.

        WIN

        Sink every enemy hull before yours goes down. A sunk hull is revealed burning on the water, so you always know what is still out there.

        MULTIPLAYER: connections are direct peer-to-peer. The host shares a battle code or invite link, the challenger links up, and the host sets the rules of engagement. No account, no server, no spectators.
